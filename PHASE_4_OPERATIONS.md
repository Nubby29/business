# Phase 4 — Lead System & Sales Pipeline

## Goal

Turn website traffic and targeted outreach into a controlled sales pipeline that can be measured from first contact through recurring revenue.

## Implemented foundation

- CRM field schema
- Defined pipeline stages
- Sales operating procedure
- Personalized outreach templates
- CSV starter tracker
- Qualification and proposal gates

## Before real outreach

Owner approval is required for:

- Final business name
- Business contact channel
- Outreach accounts
- Prospect lists/data sources
- Sending volume
- Any paid lead source
- Any customer commitment

## First operating target

Start with a small, manually reviewed batch of prospects rather than mass outreach.

For every prospect:

1. Identify a real or plausible repetitive workflow.
2. Record the prospect in the tracker.
3. Personalize one outreach message.
4. Record the date and channel.
5. Record the response.
6. Move the prospect through the pipeline.
7. Measure which workflow categories produce meaningful conversations.

## Automation boundary

Phase 4 establishes the system and templates. Automated sending should not be enabled by default. The first outreach batches should remain human-reviewed so messaging, targeting, compliance, and positioning can be corrected before scaling.
