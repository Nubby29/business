# Brand Guidelines

## Identity
AI Business Systems is the provisional brand name. The identity communicates practical business automation rather than AI hype.

## Logo
The primary logo is a wordmark paired with the ABS workflow mark in assets/logo.svg.

The mark uses three connected nodes to represent a workflow moving from input to process to outcome.

## Clear space
Keep at least the height of the mark between the logo and surrounding content.

## Minimum size
- Full logo: 120px wide on digital surfaces.
- Icon mark: 24px digital minimum where legibility permits.

## Backgrounds
Preferred: white, very light neutral, or Ink 950 for reversed logo use.

Avoid placing the logo on busy images or low-contrast backgrounds.

## Voice
Write like a capable technical partner explaining a business problem clearly.

Do: be specific, explain what changes, state assumptions, use plain business language, and show evidence when available.

Do not: use AI buzzwords without a concrete function, invent results, imply guaranteed savings, or create artificial urgency.

## Public proof
Only publish real customer work, approved testimonials, and measured outcomes. Until then, use clearly labeled examples or demonstrations.

## Naming rule
Do not lock domains, social handles, legal registrations, or paid accounts to the provisional name without owner approval.
