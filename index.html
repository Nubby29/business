FAQ

Before we automate anything.

Do I need to know exactly what should be automated?

No. You only need to explain the repetitive task and the result you want. The audit helps identify the best automation opportunity.

Can you work with our existing tools?

Usually. The implementation is scoped around the systems and access available to the business, including web tools, spreadsheets, APIs, databases, and notification systems.

Will you replace our whole system?

Not by default. The goal is to improve an existing workflow with the smallest practical change that produces a useful result.

What about ongoing support?

Monthly maintenance can cover monitoring, fixes, integration changes, scheduled reports, and small improvements. Scope is defined by the selected plan.
