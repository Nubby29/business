# AI Business Systems

AI-assisted business automation company owned by Jhave.

## Mission

Help small and medium businesses eliminate repetitive manual work by designing, building, and maintaining practical automation systems.

## Initial offer

**Automation Audit → Implementation → Monthly Operations**

### Starting pricing

- Automation Audit: ₱2,500–₱5,000
- Starter Automation: ₱10,000–₱20,000
- Business Automation System: ₱25,000–₱60,000+
- Monthly Maintenance & Operations: ₱2,500–₱15,000/month

Pricing is a launch framework and will be refined using real delivery and sales data.

## Operating model

Jhave is the owner. ChatGPT acts as the AI operating layer for research, product development, marketing, sales support, documentation, analysis, and operational workflows. Human approval remains required for consequential financial, legal, account, advertising, and customer commitments.
