# Offer & Pricing

## Core promise

We find repetitive work inside your business and build practical systems that automate it.

The service is designed for businesses that already have working processes but spend too much time copying data, checking websites, preparing reports, sending repetitive messages, or moving information between tools.

## Ideal customer profile

Initial focus:

- Recruiting and staffing companies
- Local service businesses
- Professional-service companies
- Businesses receiving repetitive web leads or forms
- Businesses producing recurring operational reports

### Common symptoms

A prospect is a strong fit when they:

- Repeat the same task every day or week
- Move information manually between systems
- Depend on spreadsheets for recurring workflows
- Need recurring website or system checks
- Send repetitive notifications or reports
- Lose time because systems do not communicate with each other

## Packages

### 1. Automation Audit — ₱2,500–₱5,000

A focused review of one business workflow.

Deliverables:

- Workflow discovery
- Manual-step map
- Automation opportunities
- Recommended tools and architecture
- Estimated implementation scope
- Prioritized action plan

Typical delivery: 2–3 business days.

The audit does not include implementation unless separately agreed.

### 2. Starter Automation — ₱10,000–₱20,000

A focused automation for one repetitive workflow.

Examples:

- Lead/form processing
- Scheduled reporting
- Website monitoring
- Spreadsheet synchronization
- Notifications
- Data collection and validation

Typical delivery: 3–7 business days after requirements are approved.

### 3. Business Automation System — ₱25,000–₱60,000+

A larger workflow involving multiple steps, systems, integrations, dashboards, or automation rules.

Possible components:

- Multiple integrations
- Database or spreadsheet workflows
- Monitoring
- Scheduled jobs
- Notifications
- Reporting dashboards
- Error handling
- Documentation
- Deployment

Timeline depends on scope and integration requirements.

### 4. Monthly Maintenance & Operations — ₱2,500–₱15,000/month

Ongoing support after implementation.

May include:

- Monitoring
- Bug fixes
- Workflow changes
- API/integration maintenance
- Scheduled reports
- Backup checks
- Performance checks
- Small feature changes
- AI/API usage management

Scope is defined by the selected monthly plan.

## Sales model

Primary funnel:

**Free/low-friction conversation → Automation Audit → Implementation → Monthly Operations**

The audit is the main paid entry product. It gives the customer a concrete workflow analysis before a larger implementation commitment.

## Qualification

Prioritize conversations where:

1. The problem is repetitive.
2. The process happens frequently enough to matter.
3. The current process is understandable and measurable.
4. The business has authority to approve the change.
5. The expected time/error reduction justifies the project cost.

## Scope protection

Every implementation proposal should explicitly define:

- Included workflows
- Supported systems
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Customer responsibilities
- Revision limits
- Third-party/API costs
- Hosting costs
- Post-launch support

Custom integrations, major redesigns, and new workflows outside the agreed scope are quoted separately.

## Launch pricing note

These prices are starting prices, not permanent prices. After the first several projects, pricing should be adjusted using actual delivery time, customer demand, conversion rate, support load, and recurring revenue.
