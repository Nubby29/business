# Marketing Plan

## Positioning

Lead with business outcomes: less repetitive work, fewer manual errors, faster operations, and better visibility.

## Website content

Explain the problem, target customers, automation examples, process, starting prices, deliverables, FAQ, and contact action.

## Content themes

- What should a business automate first?
- Manual spreadsheet workflows
- Automated reporting
- Website monitoring
- Form-to-notification workflows
- Lead routing
- Connecting existing business tools
- Automation mistakes to avoid
- Before/after workflow examples

## Proof strategy

Use real, permission-appropriate examples from existing technical work. Each case study should show: Problem -> Manual process -> Automation -> Result.

Do not claim customer results that have not been measured or verified.

## Outreach

Start with small, targeted batches. Identify a plausible repetitive workflow, write a personalized observation, offer a short discovery conversation, avoid exaggerated claims, and track responses.

## Launch metrics

Track prospects contacted, responses, discovery calls, audits sold, implementations sold, revenue, recurring revenue, delivery time, and lead source.

Paid promotion should wait until the landing page, contact workflow, offer, pricing, proof, and lead-response process are working. Any advertising spend requires owner approval.
