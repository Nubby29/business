# Sales Pipeline Operating Procedure

## Daily operating loop

1. Review new leads.
2. Assign each lead a pipeline stage.
3. Add the next action and date.
4. Respond to qualified inbound leads.
5. Send only targeted outreach that follows the outreach rules.
6. Record replies and objections.
7. Move qualified opportunities into discovery.
8. Prepare an audit proposal when the workflow is sufficiently understood.
9. Review overdue follow-ups.
10. Record the day's activity.

## Qualification gate

Before proposing implementation, confirm:

- Repetitive workflow exists.
- Frequency is sufficient to matter.
- Current process is understandable.
- Desired outcome is defined.
- Required access is possible.
- Scope can be described.
- Customer has authority or access to the decision maker.

## Proposal gate

Do not send an implementation proposal until the workflow, deliverables, timeline, customer responsibilities, third-party costs, and acceptance criteria are documented.

## Won gate

A deal enters **Won** only after the customer's commitment and agreed commercial terms are confirmed.

## Handoff to delivery

Create a delivery record containing:

- Customer
- Project scope
- Requirements
- Access needed
- Milestones
- Acceptance criteria
- Support plan
- Risks
- Owner-approved communication channel

## Weekly metrics

- New prospects
- New conversations
- Discovery calls
- Audits proposed
- Audits sold
- Implementations proposed
- Implementations sold
- Revenue
- Recurring revenue
- Average sales cycle
- Delivery hours
- Lost reasons
