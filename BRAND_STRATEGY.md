# Brand Strategy

## Provisional brand
**AI Business Systems**

This remains a provisional name until the owner approves a final business name after availability and domain checks.

## Positioning
We find repetitive work inside a business and build practical systems that automate it.

The company should be positioned around the business outcome and workflow, not around a specific AI model or technology stack.

## Brand promise
**Turn repetitive business work into reliable systems.**

## Suggested tagline
**Practical automation for growing businesses.**

## Brand personality
- Professional — trustworthy enough for operational workflows.
- Practical — focused on useful outcomes rather than hype.
- Technical — capable of building real integrations and software.
- Approachable — understandable to non-technical business owners.
- Modern — uses automation and AI where they provide measurable value.

## Messaging principles
Lead with the problem. Prefer “Stop doing repetitive work manually.” over generic AI-industry claims.

Explain outcomes plainly: fewer repetitive tasks, less manual data entry, more consistent reporting, faster notifications, better workflow visibility, and fewer missed operational checks.

Never claim a percentage saved, customer result, testimonial, or case study unless it is supported by real work.

## Initial audience
1. Recruiting / staffing
2. Local service businesses
3. Professional services

## Visual direction
Communicate business operations + technology + reliability.

Avoid generic robot mascots, glowing AI brains, excessive gradients, stock-photo-heavy layouts, and hype-driven AI imagery.

Favor geometric workflow/connection symbolism, strong typography, restrained color usage, generous spacing, clear hierarchy, and subtle technical details.

## Brand architecture
- Automation Audit
- Starter Automation
- Business Automation Systems
- Monthly Operations

AI is a capability inside the business, not the entire identity.

## Approval gate
The owner must approve the final name, public contact details, domain, and any external account before launch.
