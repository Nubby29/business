# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do prospects need to know what to automate?
No. The first conversation should focus on the repetitive task, frequency, current tools, and desired outcome.

## What kinds of workflows are a good fit?
High-frequency, rule-based work with measurable time savings or error reduction is the strongest fit.

## Can existing systems remain in place?
Yes. The default approach is to improve the current workflow rather than replace an entire system.

## What is included in monthly operations?
Depending on the selected plan: monitoring, bug fixes, integration maintenance, scheduled reports, backup checks, performance checks, and small feature changes.

## What is not promised?
No guaranteed business outcome, unlimited revisions, unlimited support, or third-party service availability. Those items must be scoped per project.
