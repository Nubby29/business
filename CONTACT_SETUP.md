# Contact & Launch Setup

The website is intentionally launch-safe while owner contact details are still undecided.

## Required before public launch

- Final business/brand name
- Owner-approved business email
- Optional phone/booking channel
- Lead capture provider or approved email workflow
- Privacy notice appropriate to the chosen capture method

## Current website behavior

The inquiry form validates required fields and prepares a structured inquiry in the browser. It does not claim to submit data to a remote service.

## Final connection

Once the owner supplies an approved destination, replace the local preparation behavior with the selected capture workflow and test it end-to-end.

Do not invent an email address, phone number, testimonial, client logo, customer result, or legal claim.
