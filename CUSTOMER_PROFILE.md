# Initial Customer Profile

## Primary market

Small and medium businesses with repetitive administrative or operational workflows.

## First outreach segments

### Recruiting & staffing

Potential problems:

- Candidate and job data moving between systems
- Website/job-page checks
- Repetitive reporting
- Form processing
- Notifications
- Spreadsheet maintenance

### Local service businesses

Potential problems:

- Lead intake
- Appointment/request routing
- Follow-up reminders
- Recurring reports
- Customer notifications
- Spreadsheet-based operations

### Professional services

Potential problems:

- Client intake
- Document collection
- Recurring reports
- Email workflows
- Data synchronization
- Internal status tracking

## Qualification questions

1. What task do you repeat most often?
2. How many times does it happen each week?
3. How long does one occurrence take?
4. Which tools are involved?
5. Where do mistakes or delays happen?
6. What happens when the task is missed?
7. Who currently performs it?
8. What would you want the system to do automatically?

## Good lead signals

A prospect is worth a discovery conversation when they describe:

- A repeated manual task
- Multiple tools that do not connect
- A recurring report
- A high-volume form or lead process
- Frequent operational checks
- A measurable delay or error

## Poor-fit signals

Do not force automation when:

- The process happens too rarely to justify implementation
- Requirements are undefined
- The customer expects unlimited changes for a fixed price
- Required third-party access cannot be provided
- The requested system has unacceptable legal, security, or compliance requirements
- The expected benefit is too small compared with project cost

## Initial customer outcome

The sales conversation should focus on measurable outcomes such as:

- Hours saved
- Faster response
- Fewer manual errors
- More reliable reporting
- Better operational visibility
- Reduced repetitive workload
