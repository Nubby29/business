# Business Plan — Phase 1

## Problem
Many small businesses still perform repetitive work manually across email, spreadsheets, forms, websites, and messaging systems.

## Solution
We build practical automation systems around the customer's existing workflow.

## Target customers
Initial focus:
- Recruiters and staffing businesses
- Local service businesses
- Small professional-service companies
- Businesses receiving repetitive web leads/forms
- Businesses producing recurring reports

## Flagship offer
### Automation Audit
We map a workflow, identify repetitive tasks, estimate automation opportunities, and provide an implementation plan.

### Implementation
We build the agreed workflow using web applications, APIs, databases, automation, AI where useful, and monitoring.

### Monthly Operations
We monitor and maintain the system, handle small changes, and provide recurring reports.

## Positioning
We sell business outcomes rather than generic AI:
**less repetitive work, fewer manual errors, faster response, and better visibility.**

## Launch economics
Initial pricing is intentionally accessible while the company builds case studies. Prices should increase when evidence shows that customers receive measurable value and delivery remains profitable.

## Success metrics
- Qualified leads
- Sales conversations
- Proposals sent
- Customers acquired
- Revenue
- Monthly recurring revenue
- Gross margin
- Customer retention
- Average delivery time
- Hours of manual work eliminated

## Phase 1 objective
Establish the business foundation and prepare a public-facing offer that can be tested with real prospects.
