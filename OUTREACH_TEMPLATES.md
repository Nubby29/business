# Initial Outreach Templates

These templates are starting points. Personalize the workflow observation before sending.

## Template A — Workflow observation

Subject: Quick automation idea for {{company}}

Hi {{name}},

I noticed {{specific workflow/observation}}.

If that process is still being handled manually, there may be a straightforward way to reduce the repetitive steps without replacing your existing tools.

I build practical business automations for things like forms, reporting, monitoring, notifications, and data workflows.

Would you be open to a short conversation about how that process works today?

— Jhave

## Template B — Reporting workflow

Subject: Question about your recurring reports

Hi {{name}},

Quick question: is {{report/task}} still prepared manually each {{week/month}}?

I work on small automation systems that collect, validate, format, and deliver recurring operational reports.

If useful, I can first map the current process and identify what could be automated.

— Jhave

## Template C — Follow-up

Hi {{name}},

Just following up on my note about {{workflow}}.

No pressure if automation is not a priority right now. If it is, I can help map the current process and identify the smallest useful improvement.

— Jhave

## Sending rules

- Personalize every message.
- Use only legitimate, permission-appropriate contact channels.
- Respect opt-outs and platform rules.
- Do not claim a problem exists unless there is a reasonable basis.
- Do not use fake urgency, fake personalization, or fabricated results.
- Track each outreach attempt in the CRM.
